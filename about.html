About & privacy.

HelmetCamHeroes shares rental-karting experiences through helmet-camera videos and recorded sessions. Track names identify venues in the archive; their appearance does not imply endorsement or an official partnership.

Using the archive

Sessions can include a driver’s name or display name, venue, date, kart details and lap times. Times depend on layout, weather, traffic and equipment. Archive records and projections are informational and may contain errors. Follow the venue’s rules and marshal instructions whenever driving.

The latest-upload feed is scheduled to refresh daily. Track videos come from the session archive and can be older. Championship scoring is explained on the championship page.

Your choices

You can browse track lists, session records and lap statistics without loading optional external media or connecting a remote. The controls explain each connection before you enable it. Choices are kept in the page’s memory and reset on reload.

  • YouTube images: on the homepage, select “Load YouTube images” to retrieve preview images from Google. Select “Disable images and reload” to block them again.
  • Maps: select “Load OpenStreetMap” to request external map tiles. Select “Disable map and reload” to block them again. Track selection and session links work without tiles.
  • YouTube videos: in a session, select “Allow YouTube videos” to load the YouTube IFrame Player API and players for that page, including comparisons. Select “Disable videos and reload” to block them again. Players use youtube-nocookie.com; this still sends information to Google and can use browser storage.
  • Phone remote: select “Enable phone remote” on the session and “Connect” on the phone to pair. “Disable phone remote” or “Disconnect” stops that device’s connection. Only share a pairing link with someone you trust.
  • Location: “Near me” asks separately for browser location permission after you enable the map. Coordinates stay in page memory, but requesting tiles around them can reveal the viewed area to OpenStreetMap. New map views are not saved in browser storage.

Reloading resets optional connections on that page. Disabling cannot undo information already sent to a provider or delete cookies or storage the provider has already set. You can remove that storage using your browser’s site-data settings. External links open the linked service under its own terms and privacy policy.

Information used when you visit

The site uses GitHub Pages and Cloudflare to deliver its files. Hosting and network providers receive request information such as IP address, browser details, requested address and time for delivery and security. Their policies describe processing and retention. Hosting logs are separate from the optional connections below. This site’s source does not include advertising or visitor-analytics scripts; provider-side settings still need verification by the operator.

Fonts and browser libraries are served with the site. They do not require Google Fonts, unpkg or cdnjs connections. Older navigation visit counts and saved map views are removed by the updated navigation and map pages.

  • Google / YouTube: optional images, the video API and playback send connection and media-request information to Google, including IP address, browser details and the requested video. YouTube may use cookies or browser storage. See Google’s privacy policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation: optional tiles send IP address, browser information, the referring site and requested map areas. See its privacy policy and map copyright and licence information.
  • PeerJS and WebRTC services: optional pairing uses PeerJS cloud signaling and STUN connection services. These receive connection information, including IP addresses and pairing identifiers. Paired devices can learn each other’s IP addresses and exchange playback commands, position and lap statistics. The feature does not request camera or microphone access. See the PeerJS project.
  • Hosting: see GitHub’s privacy statement and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Some providers operate outside the European Economic Area. Their policies describe international processing. The operator must confirm applicable transfer arrangements and safeguards for the services it uses; no particular transfer mechanism is asserted here.

Browser storage and local tools

The site code does not set first-party cookies. It uses browser local storage for features you request:

  • kartingViewerMode remembers a PC/TV mode you select.
  • apexbrake:… stores local video-analysis segments. flp:track:… stores track-analysis snapshots you save.
  • Administrator tools can store GitHub and YouTube API credentials in that browser when the administrator saves them. The relevant API receives the credential when used.

These values remain until removed through the feature or your browser’s site-data controls. Local analysis files are processed in your browser by the included tools. Sharing or exporting a file is a separate action. Clearing site data removes local preferences and saved analysis and may sign the administrator out of the local tools.

Published driver records and footage

Driver names, venue and date combinations, lap history and identifiable people in footage can be personal data, even when a surname is abbreviated. The archive is public and can be indexed or copied. Public availability of a result or video alone does not establish permission to republish it.

The operator needs to document the source, purpose and lawful basis for published personal data, provide information to affected people, and set appropriate retention and review criteria. Those details have not been verified. A visitor’s choice to load a video does not give permission to publish the people shown in it.

Corrections and privacy requests

You may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal data, to object, and in applicable cases to portability. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Rights depend on the processing and applicable law.

A private request contact must be added by the operator. Please do not post identity documents, contact details or sensitive information in public video comments. When contacting the operator, identify the session or video URL and the correction or privacy concern; only information necessary to handle the request should be requested.

You can complain to the supervisory authority in the EU country where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred. In Lithuania, this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (VDAI).

Content and third-party notices

Videos, images, track names and logos can be protected by copyright or other rights. Linking or embedding does not grant permission to copy or reuse them. Contact the relevant rights holder for reuse permission. Report a rights or identification concern through the private contact once supplied.
