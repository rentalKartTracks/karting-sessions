From the driver’s seat.

HelmetCamHeroes shares rental-karting experiences through helmet-camera videos. Explore the tracks we’ve visited, watch a ride, or browse recorded sessions and lap times.

Using the archive

Sessions include the driver, track, configuration and recorded lap times. Compare times on the same layout, and consider weather and kart differences. Some sessions have no available video. Championship scoring is explained on the championship page.

Updates and corrections

The latest-upload feed is refreshed daily. Track videos come from the session archive and may be older than the channel’s newest upload. To report a wrong track, broken video or session detail, leave a comment on the related YouTube video.

Privacy and external services

The homepage loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap and video thumbnails from YouTube. The session viewer loads the YouTube player only after you select “Allow YouTube videos”. This choice is held in memory for the current page. Select “Disable videos and reload” to return to blocked playback. This does not delete storage already set by YouTube; use your browser’s site-data controls to clear it. The player uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed domain. These services receive connection information, such as your IP address, when their resources load. Following a YouTube link opens YouTube.

Site files are served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Some libraries and fonts load from external providers. Navigation visit tracking has been removed. Older visit counts are deleted when the updated navigation loads.

Learn more from Google’s privacy policy, the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy, GitHub’s privacy statement, and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.
